# Interview 3 — Shanmukhi Priya
*Role:* B.Tech Student  
*Company Stage:** Student / classmate
I spoke with my classmate Shanmukhi Priya about AI tool usage patterns. She uses multiple AI tools including ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Blackbox AI depending on the task.
She explained that if one tool does not provide a good answer, she immediately switches to another tool. She also mentioned paying for Blackbox AI once for about one month but later returned to mostly free usage.

# Direct Quotes
- “I check multiple AI tools for the same problem.”
- “Different tools work better for different situations.”
- “This can help software engineers manage spending.”
# Most Surprising Thing
The surprising insight was that users do not stay loyal to one AI tool. They constantly compare outputs across multiple products.
# What Changed In My Design
This conversation helped me improve the multi-tool audit approach instead of focusing only on one AI provider. It reinforced the importance of comparing spending across an entire AI stack.
