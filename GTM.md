# GTM.md

## Go-To-Market Strategy

The AI Spend Audit project is designed for developers, startups, students, and engineering teams who use multiple AI tools and subscriptions but do not clearly understand whether they are overspending.

The primary goal of the product is to provide instant visibility into AI subscription inefficiencies and encourage users to optimize their AI spending stack.

---

## Initial Target Users

### 1. Developers
Developers using tools like:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini

Many developers subscribe to multiple AI tools simultaneously without understanding overlap or unnecessary spending.

---

### 2. Startup Teams

Small engineering teams often purchase:
- Team plans
- Business subscriptions
- API access
- multiple AI coding tools

The audit system helps identify inefficient team-plan usage and alternative workflows.

---

### 3. Students & Individual Users

Students frequently use:
- free tiers
- multiple accounts
- overlapping AI products

The project includes honest low-savings recommendations for users already spending efficiently.

---

## Distribution Strategy

Social Sharing

The project includes:
- public audit URLs
- screenshot-friendly reports
- shareable savings summaries

This encourages organic sharing between developers and teams.

# Product-Led Growth

The application does not require login before generating value.

Users can:
- instantly create audits
- receive recommendations
- share reports publicly

This reduces onboarding friction and improves conversion potential.

## Why This Product Could Work

AI subscription fragmentation is increasing rapidly.

Many users now simultaneously pay for:
- chat assistants
- coding copilots
- API subscriptions
- team collaboration AI products

The project solves a growing visibility problem:
users often do not understand where AI spending overlaps or becomes inefficient.
